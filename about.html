Mehta & Mehta is a corporate legal firm promoted by Atul Mehta and Dipti Mehta, offering Company Secretarial, Legal, Opinion & Advisory, Compliance Management, M&A, IPO, Funding, Due Diligence, FEMA, SEBI, Insolvency and ESG services from a single, PAN-India platform.

Our Story

From a Boutique CS Practice to a Full-Service Advisory Platform

Mehta & Mehta was set up in Mumbai by Atul Mehta and Dipti Mehta as a company secretarial practice. Three decades on, the firm sits at a different scale altogether — its work now runs across management advisory, mentoring, strategy, finance, legal, compliance, HR, secretarial, marketing, operations and sustainability, built one practice area at a time as clients' needs got more complex.

That range is deliberate. Founders and promoters who want to keep ownership of their business but hand day-to-day execution to professionals are exactly who this model is built for — and it's the same logic that shapes how we run an IPO mandate: one team, accountable for the whole timeline, rather than a chain of specialists who only see their slice of it.

The head office sits in Worli, Mumbai; branch offices carry that same model into 13 cities across India, with select international reach through business affiliates.

1990s

Founded in Mumbai as a company secretarial practice, with the founders directly involved in every mandate.

2000s

Legal services and a dedicated SEBI / capital-markets practice added as client work moved beyond compliance filings.

2010s

FEMA, restructuring and insolvency advisory built out, alongside the first branch offices outside Mumbai.

Recent years

Fund-services compliance (MF, AIF, PMS), ESG advisory and Social Stock Exchange advisory added as those markets matured.

Today

500+ corporate clients; 100+ professionals across 13 branch offices, PAN-India.
