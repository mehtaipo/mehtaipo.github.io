Our Specialization

End-to-End IPO Advisory — Pre, During & Post Listing

A dedicated desk that stays engaged from the first due-diligence exercise to the filing of the final prospectus, and continues supporting the company through its life as a listed entity.

Pre-IPO
  • Due diligence starts at incorporation, not once the DRHP process begins
  • Findings are documented and walked through with management and appointed counsel
  • Every risk factor with a bearing on the financials is flagged early for disclosure
  • Corrective steps are recommended before gaps become findings
  • Compounding and condonation-of-delay matters handled where they arise
  • Standing in for the company before the NCLT, Regional Director and ROC
During IPO
  • Working with management to assemble every document the ROC will ask for
  • Filing the Red Herring Prospectus with the Registrar
  • Chasing sign-off from the legal, procedural, prosecution & complaints desks — usually within a day
  • Owning the regulator query-response cycle end to end
  • Tracking GNL2 and RHP approvals through to completion
  • Filing the final Prospectus once approvals are in hand
Post-IPO
  • Clearing every filing that needs to be in place before the stock starts trading
  • Staying on call with the ROC for real-time advisory through the listing window
  • Taking on the filings the Stock Exchanges require once the company is listed
  • A team focused specifically on listed-company compliance, not a generalist bench
  • On record advising top-100 listed names such as New India Assurance & SBI Life
  • Also on record for top-500 listed companies such as Indigo Paints & Navkar Corporation
