How We Run an IPO Mandate

A public issue is really three different jobs stitched together — cleaning up the company's records before anyone else sees them, getting the paperwork through the regulator during the offer, and keeping the company compliant once it's trading. We stay on for all three, rather than handing off between stages.

Stage One

Pre-IPO: Getting the House in Order

This is the stage most firms treat as a formality — we treat it as the one that decides how smoothly everything after it goes. Due diligence starts the day we're engaged, not once a DRHP deadline is looming, so that whatever needs fixing gets fixed while there's still time to fix it quietly.

  • Due diligence run from day one of the engagement, well ahead of the DRHP timeline
  • Findings written up and walked through with management and appointed legal counsel
  • Every risk factor that could move the financials flagged for disclosure in the Prospectus
  • Corrective steps recommended before a gap turns into a regulator finding
  • Compounding and condonation-of-delay matters resolved where they exist
  • Representing the company before the NCLT, Regional Director and Registrar of Companies
  • Working alongside management to pull together every document the ROC will ask for
  • Filing the Red Herring Prospectus with the Registrar of Companies
  • Chasing no-objection sign-off from the legal, procedural, prosecution and complaints desks — typically inside a day
  • Owning every regulator query end to end, from receipt to reply
  • Tracking GNL2 and RHP approvals until they clear
  • Filing the final Prospectus once every approval is in hand
Stage Two

During IPO: Where Timelines Get Tight

Once the offer is live, delay has a cost. This is the stage where we're liaising with the ROC in real time — following up on approvals the same day queries land, rather than letting them sit in a queue while the market window narrows.

Stage Three

Post-IPO: Life as a Listed Company

Listing day isn't the finish line — it's where a different set of obligations starts. Our listed-company compliance team picks up from here, so the same firm that took you through the offer keeps you current with the Stock Exchanges afterwards.

  • Clearing every filing that has to be in place before the stock starts trading
  • Staying available to the ROC for real-time advisory through the listing window
  • Handling the ongoing filings the Stock Exchanges require once the company is listed
  • A team that specialises in listed-company compliance rather than treating it as an add-on
  • On record advising top-100 listed names such as New India Assurance and SBI Life
  • Also on record for top-500 listed companies including Indigo Paints and Navkar Corporation
Why Route It Through One Desk

One Team, Accountable for the Whole Timeline

Splitting due diligence, filing and post-listing compliance across different advisors creates handoff gaps — exactly where deadlines slip. Keeping it with one desk removes that risk.

Same-Day Query Turnaround

Legal, procedural, prosecution and complaint-section objections are chased down, typically within one working day of being raised.

Continuity Across Stages

The people who ran your due diligence are the same ones filing your RHP and the same ones handling your post-listing compliance.

Track Record at Scale

80+ IPOs and listing mandates handled to date, spanning debt listings, SME issues and some of the largest mainboard IPOs in the country.
